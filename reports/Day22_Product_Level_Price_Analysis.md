# Day 22: Product-Level Price Analysis

## 1. Objective
The objective of this task is to analyze product-level pricing and sales patterns in the Online Retail dataset.

## 2. Overview
Product-level analysis helps understand how different products contribute to overall sales. It provides insights into product demand, pricing patterns, and sales performance.

## 3. Analysis Plan
The following aspects will be considered:

- Identification of top-selling products.
- Analysis of product-wise sales quantity.
- Examination of average unit price across products.
- Comparison of product sales performance.
- Visualization of top products using charts.

## 4. Business Relevance
Product-level price analysis can help businesses understand product demand and identify products that contribute significantly to revenue. These insights can support pricing decisions, inventory planning, and business strategy.

## 5. Expected Outcome
The analysis is expected to provide a better understanding of product-level sales and pricing patterns in the Online Retail dataset.

## 6. Conclusion
This task focuses on understanding product-level pricing and sales behavior. The findings can provide useful background for the project's broader objective of studying dynamic pricing using Double Machine Learning (DML).
